Aqua-Checkin Help

Aqua-Checkin accounts for all Aqua employees & guests during an emergency evacuation. Use it from any internet-connected computer, phone, or tablet.

✅Checking in and out

  1. On the home screen, start typing your first name, last name, or department in the search box.
  2. Find your name in the list that appears.
  3. Tap IN when you're accounted for, or OUT if you're leaving or were checked in by mistake.
A badge next to a name shows their current status for today — In or Out. Duplicate taps are fine; the report always counts each person once.

➕Adding a guest

If someone isn't in the directory (a visitor, contractor, or new hire not yet listed):

  1. First, search the home screen to confirm they aren't already listed.
  2. Tap Add a guest not in the list.
  3. Enter their first and last name (and optionally a company or department), then tap Add Guest.
  4. They now appear in the list — marked Guest — and can be checked in like anyone else.

📋Roll Call Report

  • Tap View today's Roll Call Report on the home screen.
  • The report lists everyone currently checked in for today's date only.
  • To share or print it, tap Share / Print and use your browser's tools to send it by email, text, or to a printer.

🚪Evacuation route maps

  • On the first floor during an evacuation? Tap First Floor Evacuation Route.
  • On the second floor? Tap Second Floor Evacuation Route.

📱Install it on your device

Computer / Laptop

  1. Open aquacheckin.github.io in Chrome.
  2. Bookmark it, or save it as an app: Chrome menu (⋮) → Cast, save, and share / More tools → Create shortcut…, check Open as window, then Create.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open aquacheckin.github.io in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. The Aqua-Checkin icon now appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open aquacheckin.github.io in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button (⋮).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen and confirm. A shortcut is added to your home screen.
